🚀 Who We Are
Xinzhou Global International Cultural Tourism Co., Ltd. is a legally registered wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE) headquartered in Xinzhou, Shanxi, China. We are more than a tourism company — we are a global bridge connecting China with the world.
Our mission is simple: Make international travel, cultural exchange, business, and education between China and the world easier, safer, and more rewarding.

🌍 What We Offer
We provide end-to-end services for individuals, businesses, and organizations. Whether you want to visit China, do business with Chinese companies, study abroad, or learn a new language — we handle everything.

🎭 International Cultural Activities
Every year, we organize international cultural exchange activities held in Xinzhou, the headquarters of our company. These events bring together participants from all over the world.

Our flagship annual event:

Xinzhou Global Cultural Exchange Program

Each year, we welcome 40+ international participants from Africa, Europe, and beyond for a 14-day immersive experience in Xinzhou and Wutaishan.

The program includes:

Traditional Wushu training with local masters

Cultural workshops (calligraphy, tea ceremony, traditional arts)

Visits to Wutaishan, Xinzhou Ancient City, and Yanmen Pass

Business networking with local enterprises

Language exchange and cultural performances

Past and future editions include:

2027 Wutaishan Wushu Edition — Martial arts cultural immersion

2028 Traditional Arts Edition — Shanxi's intangible cultural heritage

2029 Language & Culture Edition — Multilingual exchange program

2030 Business & Trade Edition — Connecting global entrepreneurs with local industries

We welcome participants from all nations. There are no borders to culture, and we believe every person deserves to experience the beauty of China.
